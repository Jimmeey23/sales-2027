Add it to your phone

Google Calendar

  1. Get p57-offers-2027.ics (attached to the annual email, or in the repo at docs/comms-2027/calendar/).
  2. On a computer, open Google Calendar → Settings → Import & export → Import.
  3. Choose the file and a calendar (we suggest a new one called “P57 Offers 2027”), then Import.

Apple Calendar

  1. On Mac: double-click the .ics file, or File → Import.
  2. On iPhone: open the file from Mail or Files and tap “Add All”.
  3. Pick a new calendar so you can hide or delete it in one go.
