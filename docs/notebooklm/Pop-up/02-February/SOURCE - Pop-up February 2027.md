# Physique 57 India · Pop-up · February 2027 Sales Plan

**Studio:** Pop-up  
**Month:** February 2027  
**Plan year:** 2027. Every target here is for February 2027; "last year" means February 2026.

This is the complete, approved sales plan for this studio and month. It has exactly 4 offers; there are no others.

## Theme and focus

**Theme:** Red Flags, Green Flags

**Objective:** Turn Valentine’s-season traffic into first memberships through a 48-hour first-class window; recruit through members’ love letters; move February renewals onto 3–12 month plans; bring lapsed clients back with a free comeback class. Every reward is tied to a purchase or a visit.

**Strategy:** Red Flags, Green Flags.

Commercial job: Turn Valentine’s-season traffic into first memberships through a 48-hour first-class window; recruit through members’ love letters; move February renewals onto 3–12 month plans; bring lapsed clients back with a free comeback class. Every reward is tied to a purchase or a visit.

Newcomer: Love at First Plié (“One class. Butterflies. A 48-hour window to make it official.”). Newcomers who buy within two days of their first class convert at several times the rate of those who wait a week. A short window at the standard 15% replaces the mystery-class mechanic and the 50% codes, and the guest pass brings the next first-timer in.

Referral: Love Bombing (The Healthy Kind) (“Love bombing, but it’s two free classes and zero manipulation.”). Referrals convert 41.2% at Kwality, 22.6% at Bandra, 28.3% at Kenkere and 76.1% at pop-ups, versus 3.8–5.7% for website leads. The 2026 BOGO codes (70 uses, ₹3.1L given) went mostly to existing clients; this one pays out only when a genuinely new person buys.

Renewal: Put a Ring On It (“If you like it, then you should’ve put a 6-month on it.”). Members using 75%+ of their sessions renew at 82–88%, and longer plans keep them using them. The best perks sit on the longest plans; nothing is unlocked by a class count, so every renewer qualifies.

Lapsed: Closure (Finally) (“No hard feelings. One free class to talk it out.”). Lapsed clients rarely return on a discount alone; the first class back is the hurdle. A free comeback class removes it, and a 72-hour window at the standard 15% turns the visit into a purchase while the habit is fresh.

## The offers (4)

Together the offers are planned to bring in ₹64,197, 19% of the target. The rest comes from full-price sales.

### Offer 1 of 4: Love at First Plié

- **Type:** Mystery-class intro (credited)
- **Who it's for:** First-time clients (phone verified), within 48 hours of their first class
- **Headline:** One class. Butterflies. A 48-hour window to make it official.
- **What they get:** Take your first class in February, then buy within 48 hours: an 8-class pack at the standard 15% off (₹5,890) plus P57 grip socks.
- **Price:** ₹5,890
- **When:** 1 Feb–21 Feb
- **Why we're running it:** Turn Valentine’s-season first classes into first memberships within 48 hours, at the standard discount.
- **Target:** 3 sales at about ₹5,890 each, ₹17,670 in total
- **Rules:** One per phone number. Purchase within 48 hours of the first class. Standard 15% only; no stacking.
- **The data behind it:** Newcomers who buy within two days of their first class convert at several times the rate of those who wait a week. A short window at the standard 15% replaces the mystery-class mechanic and the 50% codes, and the guest pass brings the next first-timer in.

### Offer 2 of 4: Closure (Finally)

- **Type:** Pick-your-status winback
- **Who it's for:** Lapsed 61–365 days.
- **Headline:** No hard feelings. One free class to talk it out.
- **What they get:** Lapsed 61–365 days: one free comeback class. Buy within 72 hours of it and get an 8-class pack at the standard 15% off (₹5,890) with 30 extra days of validity.
- **Price:** ₹5,890
- **When:** 1 Feb–21 Feb
- **Why we're running it:** Bring lapsed clients back through one free class, then convert them at the standard lapsed 15%.
- **Target:** 2 sales at about ₹5,890 each, ₹11,780 in total
- **Rules:** Lapsed 61–365 days. One comeback class per client. Purchase within 72 hours of it. Standard lapsed 15% only; no stacking.
- **The data behind it:** Lapsed clients rarely return on a discount alone; the first class back is the hurdle. A free comeback class removes it, and a 72-hour window at the standard 15% turns the visit into a purchase while the habit is fresh.

### Offer 3 of 4: Put a Ring On It

- **Type:** Valentine’s renewal ladder
- **Who it's for:** Members renewing in February (any expiry date).
- **Headline:** If you like it, then you should’ve put a 6-month on it.
- **What they get:** Any February renewal: 3 Months at 15% (₹49,177 / ₹35,879) + a P57 mini-band “ring”; 6 Months at 15% + 1 private session (₹96,658 / ₹69,883); Annual at the standard 20% + the annual perks + your name engraved on the Wall of Commitment. Say yes at the desk and the bell rings, confetti, the class applauds. Pop-ups: 10-class pack at 10% + the mini-band (₹7,655).
- **Price:** ₹7,655
- **When:** 1 Feb–28 Feb
- **Why we're running it:** Move February renewals onto 3–12 month plans with a reason to say yes on the spot.
- **Target:** 3 sales at about ₹7,655 each, ₹22,965 in total
- **Rules:** 3M 16.4% / 6M 19.6% effective (perks at list); annual at the standard 20%. One ring per renewal. No stacking.
- **The data behind it:** Members using 75%+ of their sessions renew at 82–88%, and longer plans keep them using them. The best perks sit on the longest plans; nothing is unlocked by a class count, so every renewer qualifies.

### Offer 4 of 4: Love Bombing (The Healthy Kind)

Partner Barre on Sun 14 Feb counts as class 2.
- **Price:** ₹5,891
- **When:** 1 Feb–28 Feb
- **Why we're running it:** Turn members into recruiters; only pay when a new person buys.
- **Target:** 2 sales at about ₹5,891 each, ₹11,782 in total
- **Rules:** The friend must be new to P57 (phone verified). Member reward: +5 days per buying friend, max 15. Standard 15% for the friend only.
- **The data behind it:** Referrals convert 41.2% at Kwality, 22.6% at Bandra, 28.3% at Kenkere and 76.1% at pop-ups, versus 3.8–5.7% for website leads. The 2026 BOGO codes (70 uses, ₹3.1L given) went mostly to existing clients; this one pays out only when a genuinely new person buys.
