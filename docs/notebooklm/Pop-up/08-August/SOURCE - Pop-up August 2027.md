**Strategy:** The Excuse Amnesty.

Commercial job: August is the quietest month of the year because August has the best excuses. So this month we buy every excuse off the table: the commitment excuse, the wasted-pack excuse, the travel excuse, the prices-will-rise excuse, the I'd-feel-weird-alone excuse and the I-only-like-one-format excuse. One offer per excuse. Every reward is tied to a purchase or a visit, never to a class count or a leaderboard.

Newcomer: Dump Us Later (“Commitment issues? Flattering. Try two classes and dump us in writing if it's not love.”). Clients with 5+ visits in month one buy again at 93.5%, so the only job in August is getting the first purchase over the line. The break-up clause costs almost nothing because almost nobody uses it, and it answers the one objection the desk hears every day: what if I can't keep it up. The exit interview turns the few who do walk into the cheapest customer research we will ever run.

Winback: The Ghosting Amnesty (“You ghosted us. We've healed. Your unused classes haven't — come claim them.”). August needs 27 winbacks at Bandra alone and the plan carried no lapsed-client offer at all, so that revenue was hoping rather than planning. The real objection is not price, it is sunk cost and mild shame: I paid for classes I never used, so buying again feels stupid. Expired classes are already paid for, which makes resurrecting up to three of them a zero-cash concession that answers the exact objection. Historic winback ticket is about ₹10,134, so an 8-class pack at 15% lands where these clients already buy.

Referral: Peer Pressure, But Make It Barre (“Misery loves company. Bring three and we'll call it a friendship.”). Referrals convert between 22% and 76%, and the free referral class is already the single most-claimed product on the price list — the demand is proven, the mechanic just has not been pointed at groups. Friends who start together come together, which protects month-two attendance, and paying the member only when a friend buys keeps the cost variable. The blame card is free and gets the studio named in a group chat we do not own.

Renewal: Quitting Is For Cowards (“Pause it. Freeze it. Abandon it for three weeks. Cancelling is the only thing we take personally.”). Members lapse between September and November, when wedding season, work trips and family travel break the habit rather than any dissatisfaction with the studio. A freeze keeps them paying and coming back instead of cancelling, and freeze days carry no cash cost, so the cash discount stays at the standard 15% and margin is untouched. Making unused freeze days giftable at the end of November turns leftover goodwill into trial traffic in the strongest selling month of the year.

### Offer 1 of 4: Dump Us Later

- **Type:** Credited newcomer intro with a written break-up clause
- **Who it's for:** First-time clients, phone verified.
- **Headline:** Commitment issues? Flattering. Try two classes and dump us in writing if it's not love.
- **What they get:** Paid trial at about ₹945, credited in full against the 8-class pack at 15% if bought within 72 hours (net ₹5,891). The break-up clause: any time in the first 14 days, if it is not for you, the desk converts every unused rupee into an 8-class pack with 60 days of validity. No break-up fee, no retention speech, no awkward conversation at the barre. One condition — you fill in the one-line exit card at the desk telling us exactly what we got wrong. That card is the whole price of leaving.
- **Price:** ₹5,891
- **When:** 1 Aug–21 Aug
- **Why we're running it:** Kill the commitment objection that stops August trials from buying, and harvest the real reason when someone walks.
- **Target:** 4 sales at about ₹5,891 each, ₹23,564 in total
- **Rules:** One per phone number. Credit applies within 72 hours of class 2. The switch must be requested inside 14 days of the start date and the exit card must be filled in at the desk. Standard 15% only, nothing stacks, no cash refunds at any point.
- **The data behind it:** Clients with 5+ visits in month one buy again at 93.5%, so the only job in August is getting the first purchase over the line. The break-up clause costs almost nothing because almost nobody uses it, and it answers the one objection the desk hears every day: what if I can't keep it up. The exit interview turns the few who do walk into the cheapest customer research we will ever run.

A freeze block pauses your pack for at least 3 days; ask the desk 48 hours ahead and use it any time from 1 Sep to 30 Nov, for any reason. Renew by 10 Aug and you get a third block. Any block you have not used by 30 Nov turns into guest classes for one friend who has never been, valid to 31 Dec.
- **Price:** ₹7,229
- **When:** 1 Aug–31 Aug
- **Why we're running it:** Protect the September-to-November renewal book before travel season breaks the habit.
- **Target:** 6 sales at about ₹7,229 each, ₹43,374 in total
- **Rules:** 3M at 15%, 6M at 15% with the private at list value, Annual at the standard 20%. Freeze blocks are minimum 3 days, must be requested 48 hours ahead, expire 30 Nov and have no cash value. Unused blocks convert to guest classes for one named first-timer, valid to 31 Dec 2027. Early-renewal bonus applies to renewals completed by 10 Aug.
