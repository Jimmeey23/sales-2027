## Theme and focus

**Theme:** The Pre-Solution

**Objective:** Pre-sell January and protect December renewals. At Kemps Corner: Pre-sell January; holiday freezes.

**Strategy:** December is softer than January at every studio. Sell January starts in December, give travellers a holiday freeze, and close the year with every expiring member contacted.

## The offers (5)

Together the offers are planned to bring in ₹14.5L (₹14,50,543), 41% of the target. The rest comes from full-price sales.

### Offer 1 of 5: Santa's Witness Protection Programme

- **Type:** New clients
- **Who it's for:** New clients
- **Headline:** He is checking it twice. Your form, that is.
- **What they get:** First 1-Month Unlimited at 15% off (₹17,203), starting any day up to 15 January, plus a 30-minute alignment consult with a trainer before the first class.
- **Price:** ₹17,203 (list ₹20,239)
- **When:** 1–31 Dec; start any day up to 15 Jan
