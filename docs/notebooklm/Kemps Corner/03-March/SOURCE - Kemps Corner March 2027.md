## Theme and focus

**Theme:** The Pre-Game

**Objective:** Fix the March dip: renew into Platinum, sell the Heiress Bundle and win back the lapsed. At Kemps Corner: Platinum is the key KPI: target 4 in 10 March and April expiries renewed in March.

## The offers (5)

Together the offers are planned to bring in ₹10.3L (₹10,33,910), 30% of the target. The rest comes from full-price sales.

### Offer 1 of 5: The Situationship Starter Kit

- **Type:** New clients
- **Who it's for:** First-time clients (International Women's Day push)
- **Headline:** Two weeks, no labels. Then decide if it is serious.
- **What they get:** Newcomers 2-Week Unlimited at ₹7,875 with a Starter Kit (grip socks and a 2-week plan from a trainer). Keep it casual: half (₹3,938) comes off a 1-Month Unlimited. Make it official: the full ₹7,875 comes off a 3-Month Unlimited. Buy within 7 days of the 2 weeks ending.
- **Price:** ₹7,875 (list ₹7,875)
- **When:** 1–21 Mar
- **Why we're running it:** Replace the free-trial-then-drift pattern with a paid, credited first step.
- **Target:** 8 sales at about ₹7,875 each, ₹63,000 in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Paid first visits convert far better than free ones. Keeping it casual gives half the price back; making it official gives all of it back, so the bigger commitment is the obvious choice.

- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** The free class removes the awkwardness of coming back; the weekly credit is earned only by rebuilding a 3-a-week habit and can only be spent on a second purchase.
