# Physique 57 India · Kemps Corner · June 2027 Sales Plan

**Studio:** Kemps Corner  
**Month:** June 2027  
**Plan year:** 2027. Every target here is for June 2027; "last year" means June 2026.

This is the complete, approved sales plan for this studio and month. It has exactly 5 offers; there are no others.

## Theme and focus

**Theme:** Out of Office, Not Out of Shape

**Objective:** Freeze, don't leave: protect members through summer travel. At Kemps Corner: Freeze, don't lose: travellers are the risk.

**Strategy:** May and June are below the monthly average in Mumbai because clients travel. Freezes keep them on the books; summer newcomers are moved straight to 3-Month commitments. Father's Day (20 Jun) opens a new audience.

## The offers (5)

Together the offers are planned to bring in ₹15.5L (₹15,51,618), 44% of the target. The rest comes from full-price sales.

### Offer 1 of 5: The Newcomers Honey Trap

- **Type:** New clients
- **Who it's for:** First-time clients
- **Headline:** Sweet start. Sticky ending. You will not want to leave.
- **What they get:** Newcomers 2-Week Unlimited at ₹7,875 with a named trainer and a written 2-week plan on day one. Buy a 3-Month Unlimited within 7 days of the 2 weeks ending and the full ₹7,875 comes off it.
- **Price:** ₹7,875 (list ₹7,875)
- **When:** 1–30 Jun
- **Why we're running it:** Move summer newcomers straight to 3-Month commitments.
- **Target:** 13 sales at about ₹7,875 each, ₹1L (₹1,02,375) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** The full credit only exists if they commit for 3 months, so the intro price works as a deposit. The 2-week plan on day one gives every newcomer a named trainer and a reason to book their next five classes.

### Offer 2 of 5: Euro Summer Eviction Notice

- **Type:** Win-back
- **Who it's for:** Clients lapsed 60+ days, especially those who left over summer travel
- **Headline:** Your villa lease is up. Your studio lease is not.
- **What they get:** 3-Month Unlimited at 15% off (₹49,177) plus 1 virtual private to use while away, and 1 extra freeze attempt.
- **Price:** ₹49,177 (list ₹57,855)
- **When:** 1–30 Jun
- **Why we're running it:** Win back travellers onto a 3-month product they can pause and use remotely.
- **Target:** 3 sales at about ₹49,177 each, ₹1.5L (₹1,47,530) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Most summer lapses are travel, not dissatisfaction. A virtual private and an extra freeze remove the reason they left.

### Offer 3 of 5: A Sanctioned Restraining Order

- **Type:** Renewal
- **Who it's for:** Members expiring in June and July
- **Headline:** Legally, you cannot leave us this summer. It is in the paperwork.
- **What they get:** Renew on a 3-Month or longer at full price (₹57,855 for a 3-Month) and get 2 extra freeze attempts, a 21-day summer freeze on top of the standard limit, 7 extra days, and 1 virtual private.
- **Price:** ₹57,855 (list ₹57,855)
- **When:** 1–30 Jun
- **Why we're running it:** Keep travellers on the books instead of letting them lapse.
- **Target:** 10 sales at about ₹57,855 each, ₹5.8L (₹5,78,550) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Freezes cost nothing and stop the summer churn. The virtual private keeps them training while away, which is what brings them back in July.

### Offer 4 of 5: Big Glute Energy (Unregulated)

- **Type:** Revenue boost
- **Who it's for:** 1-Month members with at least 7 days left
- **Headline:** Your 1-Month is a phase. Six months is a personality.
- **What they get:** Upgrade mid-month to a 6-Month Unlimited at 12% off (₹1,00,069): the unused days on your 1-Month come off in full, plus 1 private session and the Physique 57 hoodie.
- **Price:** ₹1,00,069 (list ₹1,13,715)
- **When:** 1–30 Jun
- **Why we're running it:** Move 1-Month members onto 6-Month plans before the summer-travel drop-off.
- **Target:** 7 sales at about ₹1,00,069 each, ₹7L (₹7,00,483) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** June and July churn comes from 1-Month members who skip a month while travelling. A 6-Month with the standard freezes keeps them through it. Unused days are their own money, not a discount.

### Offer 5 of 5: Dad Bod Intervention

- **Type:** Flash sale
- **Who it's for:** Members buying for a father, partner or brother new to the studio
- **Headline:** He says he is "built different". Prove it.
- **What they get:** A 4-class pack gift for him at 20% off (₹5,670). If he books his first class within 14 days, the member earns on the referral ladder (1 bonus class).
- **Price:** ₹5,670 (list ₹7,088)
- **When:** 19–20 Jun (Father's Day weekend, 48 hours)
- **Why we're running it:** Open a new audience through members.
- **Target:** 4 sales at about ₹5,670 each, ₹22,680 in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Men are a small share of clients; a gift from a member is the warmest way in. The member's referral reward is earned only when he shows up.
