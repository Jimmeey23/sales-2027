## Theme and focus

**Theme:** Black Friday Hostage Crisis

**Objective:** Sell commitment length in a short, sharp window. At Kemps Corner: Smallest month of the year: early renewals to 6-Month protect December.

**Strategy:** Black Friday (26 Nov) to Cyber Monday (29 Nov) is a 4-day window for bonus-class packs and the prepaid credit vault, with no blanket membership discounts. Early renewals in November protect December, the weakest renewal month — rewards rise with commitment.

- **Who it's for:** First-time clients only
- **Headline:** Buy 10, Get 12 — Your Habit, No 3-Month Commitment
- **What they get:** Buy a 10-Class Pack at ₹19,425 and get 2 bonus classes: 12 classes for the price of 10, with 60-day validity. First-time clients only.
- **Price:** ₹19,425 (list ₹19,425)
- **When:** 26–29 Nov (Black Friday to Cyber Monday)
- **Why we're running it:** Sell 3-month first purchases in a short, sharp window.
- **Target:** 10 sales at about ₹19,425 each, ₹1.9L (₹1,94,250) in total

- **The data behind it:** Straightforward acquisition: creates enough studio visits to build a habit without asking for a 3-month commitment. Replaces the 3-Month countdown discount.
