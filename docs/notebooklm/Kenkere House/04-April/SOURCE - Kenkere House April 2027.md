## The offers (4)

Together the offers are planned to bring in ₹5.7L (₹5,73,210), 29% of the target. The rest comes from full-price sales.

### Offer 1 of 4: Reckless Impulse Decision

- **Type:** New clients
- **Who it's for:** First-time clients, within 9 hours of their first class
- **Headline:** Bad decisions make good stories. This one comes with confetti.
- **What they get:** Buy a 3-Month Unlimited within 9 hours of your first class at 18% off (₹34,612 instead of ₹42,210), hit the Impulse Buzzer at the front desk (confetti cannon included), and take home the anniversary tee plus a 30-minute trainer consult to plan your first 12 weeks.
- **Price:** ₹34,612 (list ₹42,210)
- **When:** 1–30 Apr
- **Why we're running it:** Sell newcomers a 3-month first purchase on the day they feel best.
- **Target:** 6 sales at about ₹34,612 each, ₹2.1L (₹2,07,672) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Endorphins fade fast: the first hours after a first class are the highest-intent moment there is. A 3-Month first purchase renews far better than a 1-Month one, and the buzzer turns every sale into a moment the whole class sees.

### Offer 2 of 4: Faking Your Own Death Didn’t Work

- **Type:** Win-back
- **Who it's for:** Clients lapsed 60+ days
- **Headline:** We found you. We always find you. Welcome back from the dead.
- **What they get:** Each lapsed client gets a mock obituary postcard ("Here lie the glutes of …"). Bring it in, pose at the Resurrection Booth, and get a 3-Month Unlimited at 17% off (₹35,034) plus 1 private session to rebuild form.
- **Price:** ₹35,034 (list ₹42,210)
- **When:** 12–30 Apr
- **Why we're running it:** Peak-month winback on a 3-month product instead of a pack.
- **Target:** 2 sales at about ₹35,034 each, ₹70,068 in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Humour lowers the awkwardness of coming back, and the booth photo becomes content. A private session in week one is the strongest way to rebuild confidence after a long break.

### Offer 3 of 4: Recreational Gambling (For Hamstrings)

- **Type:** Renewal
- **Who it's for:** Members renewing or upgrading to 3-Month, 6-Month or Annual
- **Headline:** Spin the wheel. Then decide: keep it, double it, or give it away.
- **What they get:** Renew or upgrade at the standard price (₹42,210 for a 3-Month) and spin the Cloud Nine wheel. Every slot pays: 2 or 3 bonus classes, 1 private, 1 virtual private, 7 extra days, 2 extra freezes, an anniversary hoodie, or the jackpot (1 private and 2 bonus classes). The twist: keep the prize, double it by taking a dare card (for example 9 classes in 14 days, or a 7 am class every week of May), or donate it to a friend as guest passes.
- **Price:** ₹42,210 (list ₹42,210)
- **When:** 5–30 Apr
- **Why we're running it:** The anniversary renewal offer: higher-value renewals with no percentage off.
- **Target:** 7 sales at about ₹42,210 each, ₹3L (₹2,95,470) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** The wheel makes a full-price renewal exciting, and the dare turns the prize into attendance in May, the month when April buyers drift. Donating brings a friend in. The cap is checked on the doubled jackpot.

### Offer 4 of 4: Moving In & Changing The Locks

- **Type:** Revenue boost
- **Who it's for:** Everyone: members, packs and newcomers
- **Headline:** Nine months. One key. No, you cannot have your old life back.
- **What they get:** The Limited Edition 9-Class Pack (an 8-class pack plus a single cost ₹10,132.50 at list), valid 99 days: ₹8,699 all April, ₹7,699 during the two 99-minute flash windows on 9 April.
- **When:** Limited Edition 9-Class Pack all April; flash price on 9 Apr from 9:09 am and 9:09 pm, 99 minutes each
- **Why we're running it:** The anniversary's entry product and its loudest moment: a flash sale everyone talks about.
- **Target:** 12 sales
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** A limited pack priced off the anniversary number is easy to say yes to for newcomers and pack buyers alike. The flash price is inside the cap; the unit target is set at the flash price, so revenue is conservative.
