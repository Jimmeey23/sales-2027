## Theme and focus

**Theme:** The Pre-Game

**Objective:** Fix the March dip: renew into Platinum, sell the Heiress Bundle and win back the lapsed. At Kenkere House: One of the two weakest months: Platinum renewals and the Heiress Bundle.

**Strategy:** March is one of the weakest months at every studio in both of the last two years, because clients wait for anniversary pricing. Platinum Tier Delusion makes waiting pointless: members who renew in March get the anniversary first (early access to the limited editions, the first numbered 9-Month keys, a reserved spot at the Golden Glute Awards). International Women's Day (8 Mar) leads the newcomer push.

## The offers (4)

Together the offers are planned to bring in ₹2L (₹2,02,242), 20% of the target. The rest comes from full-price sales.

### Offer 1 of 4: The Situationship Starter Kit

- **Type:** New clients
- **Who it's for:** First-time clients (International Women's Day push)
- **Headline:** Two weeks, no labels. Then decide if it is serious.
- **What they get:** Newcomers 2-Week Unlimited at ₹6,038 with a Starter Kit (grip socks and a 2-week plan from a trainer). Keep it casual: half (₹3,019) comes off a 1-Month Unlimited. Make it official: the full ₹6,038 comes off a 3-Month Unlimited. Buy within 7 days of the 2 weeks ending.
- **Price:** ₹6,038 (list ₹6,038)
- **When:** 1–21 Mar
- **Why we're running it:** Replace the free-trial-then-drift pattern with a paid, credited first step.
- **Target:** 6 sales at about ₹6,038 each, ₹36,228 in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Paid first visits convert far better than free ones. Keeping it casual gives half the price back; making it official gives all of it back, so the bigger commitment is the obvious choice.

### Offer 2 of 4: The Glute Walk of Shame

- **Type:** Win-back
- **Who it's for:** Clients lapsed 60+ days
- **Headline:** Same outfit as when you left. No questions asked. Every week you stay, we pay you.
- **What they get:** First comeback class free, no questions asked. Then a 1-Month Unlimited at full price (₹14,595); every week with 3 or more classes earns ₹600 in credit, up to ₹2,400, spent on the next membership or pack.
- **Price:** ₹14,595 (list ₹14,595)
- **When:** 1–31 Mar
- **Why we're running it:** Full-price comebacks in the weakest month, with the second purchase built in.
- **Target:** 4 sales at about ₹14,595 each, ₹58,380 in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** The free class removes the awkwardness of coming back; the weekly credit is earned only by rebuilding a 3-a-week habit and can only be spent on a second purchase.

### Offer 3 of 4: Platinum Tier Delusion

- **Type:** Renewal
- **Who it's for:** Members expiring in March and April
- **Headline:** You are not on the guest list. You ARE the guest list.
- **What they get:** Renew on a 3-Month Unlimited at 15% off (₹35,878) plus 9 extra days and 1 extra freeze, and get Platinum status for the anniversary: 48-hour early access to the April limited editions (the 9-Month Unlimited and the 9-Class Pack), first pick of numbered 9-Month keys, a reserved spot at the Golden Glute Awards, and the first spins of the wheel.
- **Price:** ₹35,878 (list ₹42,210)
- **When:** 1–31 Mar
- **Why we're running it:** Stop March renewals waiting for April by giving March renewers the best seat at the anniversary.
- **Target:** 3 sales at about ₹35,878 each, ₹1.1L (₹1,07,634) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** March is one of the weakest months because clients hold out for anniversary deals. Platinum makes renewing in March the way to get the anniversary first, so waiting buys nothing. Early access costs nothing and the limited editions sell out faster.

### Offer 4 of 4: Ides of March Panic Buy

- **Type:** Flash sale
- **Who it's for:** Pack buyers and members
- **Headline:** Beware the Ides of March. Also, beware missing this.
- **What they get:** 20-class pack at 18% off (₹20,664 instead of ₹25,200) with 9 months to use it instead of 6.
- **Price:** ₹20,664 (list ₹25,200)
- **When:** 15 Mar, 9 am to midnight (15 hours)
- **Why we're running it:** A mid-month cash spike in the softest month.
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** The longer validity removes the main reason not to buy the biggest pack, and the 15-hour window creates a reason to buy today.
