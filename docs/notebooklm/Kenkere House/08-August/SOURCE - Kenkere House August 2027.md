## The offers (4)

Together the offers are planned to bring in ₹4L (₹3,97,657), 25% of the target. The rest comes from full-price sales.

### Offer 1 of 4: Cult Recruitment & Human Sacrifice

- **Type:** New clients
- **Who it's for:** Members bringing two friends new to the studio
- **Headline:** Bring two friends. One of them may survive the planks.
- **What they get:** The two friends each buy an 8-class pack within 72 hours at 12% off (₹7,669) plus 1 guest pass. The recruiter earns on the referral ladder: 1 bonus class for each friend who tries, 7 extra days (or 2 bonus classes on a pack) for each friend who buys, and a free private session when both buy. Recruiters also score race checkpoints.
- **Price:** ₹7,669 (list ₹8,715)
- **When:** 1–15 Aug (Friendship Day is 1 Aug)
- **Why we're running it:** Turn the race month into the best referral month of the second half.
- **Target:** 15 sales at about ₹7,669 each, ₹1.2L (₹1,15,035) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Friends who join together stay together, and the referral ladder rewards members for buyers, not just trial visitors.

### Offer 2 of 4: Shame-Fueled Competitive Comeback

- **Type:** Win-back
- **Who it's for:** Clients lapsed 60+ days
- **Headline:** Everyone else has a head start. Good. Use the shame.
- **What they get:** Come-back 1-Month Unlimited at 15% off (₹12,406) with an Amazing Race 2.0 entry, a 9-point comeback head start and 1 guest pass. Finish in the studio's top 20 and half of what you paid (₹6,203) comes off a 3-Month Unlimited or bigger.
- **Price:** ₹12,406 (list ₹14,595)
- **When:** 1–31 Aug
- **Why we're running it:** Win back lapsed clients through the race and turn their finish into a 3-Month.
- **Target:** 4 sales at about ₹12,406 each, ₹49,624 in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** The race gives lapsed clients a reason and a deadline. The comeback head start keeps it fair, and the refund pays out only to clients who finish in the top 20.

### Offer 3 of 4: A Quickie in the Pit Stop

- **Type:** Renewal
- **Who it's for:** Members expiring in August and September
- **Headline:** Ten minutes. In and out. You will be back on the track before anyone notices.
- **What they get:** Renew at the Pit Stop table straight after class, in under 10 minutes: 3-Month Unlimited at 8% off (₹38,833) plus 7 extra days and double race points for the week.
- **Price:** ₹38,833 (list ₹42,210)
- **When:** 1–31 Aug
- **Why we're running it:** Make renewing the easiest thing a member does all month.
- **Target:** 6 sales at about ₹38,833 each, ₹2.3L (₹2,32,998) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Most renewals are lost to delay, not to price. A table, a timer and a small reward for renewing on the spot turn the decision into a race moment.

### Offer 4 of 4: The Amazing Race Cover Story

- **Type:** Revenue boost
- **Who it's for:** Everyone at the finale and online
- **Headline:** Your face. A magazine cover. A whole year of earning it.
- **What they get:** 20-class pack at 18% off (₹20,664) with a finisher medal and 9 months to use it instead of 6.
- **When:** 31 Aug, 9 pm to midnight (3 hours)
- **Why we're running it:** Convert the finale crowd, including friends and spectators, on the night.
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Finale night has the most people and the most energy in the studio all year. A 3-hour window sells to them before the feeling fades.
