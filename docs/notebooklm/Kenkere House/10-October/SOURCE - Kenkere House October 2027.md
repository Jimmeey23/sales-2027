# Physique 57 India · Kenkere House · October 2027 Sales Plan

**Studio:** Kenkere House  
**Month:** October 2027  
**Plan year:** 2027. Every target here is for October 2027; "last year" means October 2026.

This is the complete, approved sales plan for this studio and month. It has exactly 4 offers; there are no others.

## Theme and focus

**Theme:** Gift of Strength

**Objective:** Use Diwali gifting to bring in new clients and keep members through the holidays. At Kenkere House: Gifting; Diwali freezes.

**Strategy:** Diwali (29 Oct 2027) pulls clients away. Gift cards turn members into recruiters; festive freezes stop members drifting; corporate packs bring in bulk prepaid trials.

## The offers (4)

Together the offers are planned to bring in ₹2.8L (₹2,80,163), 21% of the target. The rest comes from full-price sales.

### Offer 1 of 4: Mothers I'd Like to Tuck

- **Type:** New clients
- **Who it's for:** Members buying gifts; recipients new to the studio
- **Headline:** Nothing says "I love you" like "I booked you a 7 am class".
- **What they get:** Buy a 1-Month Unlimited gift card at ₹14,595: the buyer gets 1 bonus class, and the recipient gets a 30-minute trainer consult when they book their first class within 14 days.
- **Price:** ₹14,595 (list ₹14,595)
- **When:** 1 Oct–7 Nov (Diwali is 29 Oct)
- **Why we're running it:** Turn members into recruiters through Diwali gifting.
- **Target:** 3 sales at about ₹14,595 each, ₹43,785 in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Every gift card is a new client arriving with a paid month already in hand. The consult gives the recipient a plan and a named trainer on day one.

### Offer 2 of 4: Lazy Bastard Rehab

- **Type:** Win-back
- **Who it's for:** Clients lapsed 60+ days
- **Headline:** New identity. New glutes. Nobody needs to know where you have been.
- **What they get:** 3-Month Unlimited at 15% off (₹35,878) with a "New Identity Kit" (grip socks and a tote) and a 30-minute consult to reset goals.
- **Price:** ₹35,878 (list ₹42,210)
- **When:** 1–31 Oct
- **Why we're running it:** Win back lapsed clients onto a 3-month product before the festive slowdown.
- **Target:** 1 sales at about ₹35,878 each, ₹35,878 in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** A fresh start lowers the awkwardness of coming back; the consult rebuilds the plan before the first class.

### Offer 3 of 4: Balls to the Wall

- **Type:** Renewal
- **Who it's for:** Members expiring October and November
- **Headline:** Pre-paid damage control. Use it within 14 days of the incident.
- **What they get:** Renew on a 3-Month at 5% off (₹40,100) with a free festival-week freeze and 3 "damage control" bonus classes to use in the 14 days after Diwali.
- **Price:** ₹40,100 (list ₹42,210)
- **When:** Renew by 24 Oct
- **Why we're running it:** Protect renewals over Diwali and bring members back straight after.
- **Target:** 5 sales at about ₹40,100 each, ₹2L (₹2,00,500) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** The bonus classes only work after the festival, so they bring members back in the first fortnight of November instead of drifting.

### Offer 4 of 4: FOMO, Fatso

- **Type:** Revenue boost
- **Who it's for:** Members and anyone buying an Annual
- **Headline:** Circling back on your team's mental health. Please see attached: barre.
- **What they get:** Annual Unlimited at 18% off (₹1,28,117) with a gold-foil membership card. One day only.
- **When:** Dhanteras (2 days before Diwali), 24 hours
- **Why we're running it:** An auspicious-day Annual sale in the biggest festive buying moment.
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Dhanteras is a day when clients expect to make a big purchase. An Annual at 18% off is the largest single sale the studio can make.
