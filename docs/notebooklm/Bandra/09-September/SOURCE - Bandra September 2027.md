## The offers (4)

Together the offers are planned to bring in ₹6L (₹6,01,245), 35% of the target. The rest comes from full-price sales.

### Offer 1 of 4: My Own Sugar Daddy (Funded by Glutes)

- **Type:** New clients
- **Who it's for:** New clients, bought within 7 days of the first class
- **Headline:** No strings. No sugar daddy. Just glutes that pay the rent.
- **What they get:** Your first 1-Month Unlimited at 20% off (₹16,191). 16 classes in 30 days: half (₹8,096) comes off a 3-Month Unlimited. 20 classes: the full ₹16,191 comes off a 6-Month or Annual, plus a private session to start it.
- **Price:** ₹16,191 (list ₹20,239)
- **When:** 1–30 Sep
- **Why we're running it:** Build the pre-festive member base on 3-Month and 6-Month plans that carry through Diwali.
- **Target:** 13 sales at about ₹16,191 each, ₹2.1L (₹2,10,483) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Data check (Lapsed & Renewals export, first-time 1-Month members): 44% already finish 16+ classes and 25% finish 20+; members who finish 16+ buy a 3-Month or bigger within 30 days 33% of the time, against 12% for the rest. So 16 classes earns a half refund that rewards a proven habit, and the full refund needs a real stretch (20 classes, roughly 5 a week). Cap check: the combined discount across both purchases stays at or under 25%.

### Offer 2 of 4: Partners in Crime & Joint Inflammation

- **Type:** Win-back
- **Who it's for:** Clients lapsed 60+ days
- **Headline:** Coming back alone is awkward. Coming back with an accomplice on staff is a heist.
- **What they get:** Come back on a 1-Month Unlimited at 17% off (₹16,798) with a named trainer as your partner in crime: a 15-minute check-in in week 1 and week 3, and 1 guest pass if you want company. Renew at the end of the month and get a bonus class and the "Partners in Crime" tee.
- **Price:** ₹16,798 (list ₹20,239)
- **When:** 1–30 Sep
- **Why we're running it:** Win lapsed clients back on their own, with a trainer as the accountability partner.
- **Target:** 6 sales at about ₹16,798 each, ₹1L (₹1,00,788) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Lapsed clients say they stopped because their routine or workout partner changed. A named trainer who checks in twice in the first month replaces the missing partner, so the comeback does not depend on bringing a friend.

- **When:** 1–30 Sep

- **The data behind it:** Members who renew before Diwali on a longer plan stay through it. The November consult is a built-in reason to come back after the festival.
