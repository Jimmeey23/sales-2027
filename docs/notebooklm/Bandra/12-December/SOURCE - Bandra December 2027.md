# Physique 57 India · Bandra · December 2027 Sales Plan

**Studio:** Bandra  
**Month:** December 2027  
**Plan year:** 2027. Every target here is for December 2027; "last year" means December 2026.

This is the complete, approved sales plan for this studio and month. It has exactly 4 offers; there are no others.

## Theme and focus

**Theme:** The Pre-Solution

**Objective:** Pre-sell January and protect December renewals. At Bandra: Pre-sell January; renewals by 20 Dec.

**Strategy:** December is softer than January at every studio. Sell January starts in December, give travellers a holiday freeze, and close the year with every expiring member contacted.

## The offers (4)

Together the offers are planned to bring in ₹6.8L (₹6,78,210), 39% of the target. The rest comes from full-price sales.

### Offer 1 of 4: Santa's Witness Protection Programme

- **Type:** New clients
- **Who it's for:** New clients
- **Headline:** He is checking it twice. Your form, that is.
- **What they get:** First 1-Month Unlimited at 15% off (₹17,203), starting any day up to 15 January, plus a 30-minute alignment consult with a trainer before the first class.
- **Price:** ₹17,203 (list ₹20,239)
- **When:** 1–31 Dec; start any day up to 15 Jan
- **Why we're running it:** Pre-sell January starts in December cash.
- **Target:** 10 sales at about ₹17,203 each, ₹1.7L (₹1,72,030) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** December is the smallest month and January the strongest for starts. The alignment consult makes the first class feel personal.

### Offer 2 of 4: The Return of the Unfit

- **Type:** Win-back
- **Who it's for:** Clients lapsed 60+ days
- **Headline:** All is forgiven. Even the classes you let expire.
- **What they get:** Buy a 1-Month Unlimited (₹20,239) or bigger, or a 12-class pack or bigger, and up to 3 classes that expired unused in 2026 are restored. No questions asked.
- **Price:** ₹20,239 (list ₹20,239)
- **When:** 1–31 Dec; start any day up to 15 Jan
- **Why we're running it:** Bank January comebacks in December, at full price.
- **Target:** 5 sales at about ₹20,239 each, ₹1L (₹1,01,195) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Expired classes are the most common lapsed-client grievance. Restoring them costs little and gives the comeback a reason that is not a discount.

### Offer 3 of 4: The December Retention Racket

- **Type:** Renewal
- **Who it's for:** Members expiring in December and January
- **Headline:** Doctor's orders: rest. Then come back stronger.
- **What they get:** Renew on a 3-Month or longer at full price (₹57,855 for a 3-Month) with a 21-day holiday freeze and 2 virtual privates for while you are away.
- **Price:** ₹57,855 (list ₹57,855)
- **When:** Renew by 20 Dec
- **Why we're running it:** Protect December renewals, the weakest renewal month.
- **Target:** 7 sales at about ₹57,855 each, ₹4L (₹4,04,985) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Members leave in December because they travel. A long freeze and remote privates remove the reason.

### Offer 4 of 4: Gift Card Black Market

- **Type:** Revenue boost
- **Who it's for:** Everyone
- **Headline:** Thought of you." (We did. Mostly about your posture.)
- **What they get:** 1-Month Unlimited gift card at 15% off (₹17,203), delivered by WhatsApp in minutes with a printable card.
- **When:** 24 Dec, 9 am to 9 pm (12 hours)
- **Why we're running it:** Capture last-minute gift buyers on the busiest gifting day.
- **Target:** 3 sales
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Late gift buyers want speed more than discount; instant delivery is the selling point.
