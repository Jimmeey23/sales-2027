## Theme and focus

**Theme:** Stronger in 30 2.0 + Valentine's

**Objective:** Run the members-only Stronger in 30 2.0 challenge and grow through referrals. At Bandra: Referrals over discounts.

**Strategy:** February is a short month with a habit problem: members who do not attend in February do not renew in March. Stronger in 30 2.0 (members only, 30 days) gives every member a reason to book. Valentine's and Galentine's are the natural referral moment.

## The offers (4)

Together the offers are planned to bring in ₹7.3L (₹7,34,308), 40% of the target. The rest comes from full-price sales.

### Offer 1 of 4: Libido Litmus Test

- **Type:** New clients
- **Who it's for:** Members, each bringing one new person (partner, friend, colleague)
- **Headline:** Bring a date to a 7 am barre class. If they still like you after, marry them.
- **What they get:** The date takes a free class with the member. If the date buys a first 1-Month Unlimited within 72 hours, it is 17% off (₹16,798) plus 1 guest pass to bring the member back the favour. The member earns on the referral ladder: 1 bonus class when the date tries, 7 extra days on their membership (or 2 more bonus classes on a pack) when the date buys.
- **Price:** ₹16,798 (list ₹20,239)
- **When:** 7–21 Feb (Valentine's week in the middle)
- **Why we're running it:** Turn members into recruiters in the best referral month of the first half.
- **Target:** 12 sales at about ₹16,798 each, ₹2L (₹2,01,576) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Referrals are the best-converting source at every studio (at Kenkere House about 8× the rate of paid-website leads). A reward that grows when the friend actually buys pays for results, not for introductions.

### Offer 2 of 4: Post-Breakup Revenge Tour

- **Type:** Win-back
- **Who it's for:** Clients lapsed 60+ days
- **Headline:** They moved on. Your glutes are about to move up.
- **What they get:** Come-back 1-Month Unlimited at 18% off (₹16,596) with entry to Stronger in 30 2.0 and a "Revenge Tour" tour poster on the challenge wall. Finish the challenge (20 classes in 30 days) and half of what you paid (₹8,298) comes off a 3-Month Unlimited or bigger.
- **Price:** ₹16,596 (list ₹20,239)
- **When:** 1–28 Feb
- **Why we're running it:** Win back lapsed clients through Stronger in 30 2.0 and turn the challenge habit into a 3-Month.
- **Target:** 7 sales at about ₹16,596 each, ₹1.2L (₹1,16,172) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Stronger in 30 2.0 is members-only, so a come-back membership is the ticket in. The refund pays out only to clients who finish the challenge and then commit for 3 months.

### Offer 3 of 4: The Extended Situationship Crisis

- **Type:** Renewal
- **Who it's for:** Members expiring in February and March
- **Headline:** Not ready for a year? Fine. Three more months of mixed signals.
- **What they get:** Renew on a 3-Month Unlimited at 10% off (₹52,070) plus 7 extra days and 1 extra freeze. Stronger in 30 finishers also get a virtual private session to use anywhere.
- **Price:** ₹52,070 (list ₹57,855)
- **When:** 1–28 Feb
- **Why we're running it:** Move 1-Month renewers onto 3-Month plans while the challenge keeps them attending.
- **Target:** 8 sales at about ₹52,070 each, ₹4.2L (₹4,16,560) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Members who attend in February renew in March. The virtual private is a reward for finishing the challenge and introduces privates as a habit.

### Offer 4 of 4: The Shared Custody Package

- **Type:** Revenue boost
- **Who it's for:** Anyone buying with a friend
- **Headline:** Two memberships. One shared private. A legally binding schedule for the 7 am.
- **What they get:** 10-class pack at 15% off (₹16,511) plus 1 guest pass for a friend, valid 60 days longer than a standard 10-class pack.
- **When:** 13 Feb only (24 hours)
- **Why we're running it:** A one-day spike for pack buyers in the shortest month.
- **Target:** 4 sales
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Galentine's is a friend moment, not a couple moment; the guest pass brings the friend in, and the longer validity removes the main objection to a bigger pack.
