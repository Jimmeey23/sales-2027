# Physique 57 India · Bandra · January 2027 Sales Plan

**Studio:** Bandra  
**Month:** January 2027  
**Plan year:** 2027. Every target here is for January 2027; "last year" means January 2026.

This is the complete, approved sales plan for this studio and month. It has exactly 4 offers; there are no others.

## Theme and focus

**Theme:** Resolution Season

**Objective:** Turn the January rush into members, and renew Dec–Jan expiries early. At Bandra: Recovery starts here: every Website lead called within 2 hours.

**Strategy:** January is when intent is highest and patience is lowest. Every first visit must leave with a booked second class and a 7-day decision window; every lead gets a call within 2 hours. Renewals due in January and February are contacted early so the post-holiday dip does not cost members.

## The offers (4)

Together the offers are planned to bring in ₹10.2L (₹10,21,139), 43% of the target. The rest comes from full-price sales.

### Offer 1 of 4: The January Delulu Club

- **Type:** New clients
- **Who it's for:** New clients, bought within 7 days of the first class
- **Headline:** Delulu is the solulu, but only if you show up 20 times.
- **What they get:** Your first 1-Month Unlimited at 20% off (₹16,191 instead of ₹20,239) and a Delulu Club card on the studio wall. Every class gets a sticker. At 16 classes in 30 days, half of what you paid (₹8,096) comes off a 3-Month Unlimited or bigger. At 20, ₹15,381 comes off a 3-Month, or the full ₹16,191 off a 6-Month or Annual. Credit, not cash; used within 7 days of the month ending.
- **Price:** ₹16,191 (list ₹20,239)
- **When:** 2–31 Jan
- **Why we're running it:** Turn the January first-visit spike into 3-Month and 6-Month members by paying for the habit, not for signing up.
- **Target:** 22 sales at about ₹16,191 each, ₹3.6L (₹3,56,202) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Data check (Lapsed & Renewals export, first-time 1-Month members): 44% already finish 16+ classes and 25% finish 20+; members who finish 16+ buy a 3-Month or bigger within 30 days 33% of the time, against 12% for the rest. So 16 classes earns a half refund that rewards a proven habit, and the full refund needs a real stretch (20 classes, roughly 5 a week). Cap check: the combined discount across both purchases stays at or under 25%.

### Offer 2 of 4: Texting Your Toxic Ex

- **Type:** Win-back
- **Who it's for:** Clients lapsed 60+ days
- **Headline:** It's 9 pm. It's been months. We know we shouldn't. U up?
- **What they get:** A personal 9 pm "u up?" WhatsApp from their old trainer, then ₹500 off a 1-Month Unlimited or bigger for every month they have been away, up to ₹4,000 (8 months). Away 5 months: the 1-Month is ₹17,739 instead of ₹20,239.
- **Price:** ₹16,239 (list ₹20,239)
- **When:** 2–31 Jan; the text goes out at 9 pm on 2 Jan
- **Why we're running it:** Reach the long-lapsed pool that ordinary winbacks ignore, without discounting the recently lapsed who come back anyway.
- **Target:** 7 sales at about ₹16,239 each, ₹1.1L (₹1,13,673) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** The scaled refund puts the biggest incentive on the hardest-to-win clients. A message from a trainer they know beats a broadcast. The cap is checked at the maximum, and the unit target assumes the maximum, so revenue is conservative.

### Offer 3 of 4: Premature Evacuation (Of Funds)

- **Type:** Renewal
- **Who it's for:** Members and pack holders expiring in January and February
- **Headline:** Your money leaves early. Your motivation stays. Everyone wins.
- **What they get:** The earlier you renew, the more you get. 7+ days before expiry: 2 bonus classes on an 8-class pack or bigger, or 3 extra days and 1 extra freeze on a membership. 14+ days before expiry: 3 bonus classes on a 12-class pack or bigger (₹19,688 for a 12-class pack), or, on a 3-Month or longer, 9 extra days, 1 extra freeze and a 30-minute trainer goal-setting consult. Full price, no % off.
- **Price:** ₹19,688 (list ₹19,688)
- **When:** 1–31 Jan
- **Why we're running it:** Pull renewals forward so the post-holiday drop-off does not cost members.
- **Target:** 28 sales at about ₹19,688 each, ₹5.5L (₹5,51,264) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Renewals cluster at the expiry date and members who leave it to the last day lapse more. A reward that grows with how early they commit moves the decision, protects price and gives the front desk a simple script: "the earlier, the better".

### Offer 4 of 4: New Year's Regret Window

- **Type:** Flash sale
- **Who it's for:** Anyone: new clients, lapsed clients, or members upgrading
- **Headline:** 72 hours. After that, you can regret it at full price.
- **What they get:** 3-Month Unlimited at 20% off (₹46,284 instead of ₹57,855) plus a pair of Physique 57 grip socks. Countdown clock on Instagram and at the front desk.
- **When:** 2–4 Jan (72 hours, ends 11:59 pm on 4 Jan)
- **Why we're running it:** Turn the New Year rush into 3-month commitments in the first 72 hours, before the January newcomer offer takes over.
- **Target:** 2 sales
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** The first weekend of January has the highest intent of the year. A hard 72-hour clock creates the urgency a month-long offer cannot.
