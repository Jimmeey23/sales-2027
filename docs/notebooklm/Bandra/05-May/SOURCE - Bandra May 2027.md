## The offers (4)

Together the offers are planned to bring in ₹8.3L (₹8,31,169), 46% of the target. The rest comes from full-price sales.

### Offer 1 of 4: The 16-Class Hostage Situation

- **Type:** New clients
- **Who it's for:** New clients, bought within 7 days of the first class
- **Headline:** We are holding your money hostage. Ransom: 16 classes.
- **What they get:** Your first 1-Month Unlimited at 20% off (₹16,191). A ransom note goes on your locker. Pay the ransom (16 classes in 30 days) and we release ₹12,143 (75%) off a 3-Month Unlimited, or the full ₹16,191 off a 6-Month or Annual.
- **Price:** ₹16,191 (list ₹20,239)
- **When:** 1–31 May
- **Why we're running it:** Lift trial-to-member conversion after the anniversary and move new members onto 3-Month plans before summer travel.
- **Target:** 19 sales at about ₹16,191 each, ₹3.1L (₹3,07,629) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Data check (Lapsed & Renewals export, first-time 1-Month members): 44% already finish 16+ classes and 25% finish 20+; members who finish 16+ buy a 3-Month or bigger within 30 days 33% of the time, against 12% for the rest. May uses a single ransom note: 16 classes releases the money, with the full amount reserved for a 6-Month or Annual. Cap check: the combined discount across both purchases stays at or under 25%.

### Offer 2 of 4: The “Look What You Lost” Tour

- **Type:** Win-back
- **Who it's for:** Clients lapsed 60+ days
- **Headline:** Four stops. One glow-up. Your ex will see the photos.
- **What they get:** Come back on a 1-Month Unlimited at full price (₹20,239) and get a tour pass with four stops: four different formats or trainers, one per week. Finish all four and unlock a 3-Month Unlimited at 20% off (₹46,284) plus a glow-up photo shoot in studio.
- **Price:** ₹20,239 (list ₹20,239)
- **When:** 1–31 May
- **Why we're running it:** Bring lapsed clients back at full price and pre-sell the 3-Month on the way out.
- **Target:** 5 sales at about ₹20,239 each, ₹1L (₹1,01,195) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Four different formats or trainers in four weeks rebuilds the habit and finds the class they will stay for. The 3-Month reward is only unlocked by finishing the tour.

### Offer 3 of 4: No Graceful Exit Strategy

- **Type:** Renewal
- **Who it's for:** April buyers on a 1-Month or pack with 12+ classes in their first 30 days
- **Headline:** You came for the party. You are staying for the cleanup.
- **What they get:** Upgrade to a 3-Month Unlimited at 10% off (₹52,070), keeping every day left on the current product, plus 1 private session and 2 extra freezes for summer.
- **Price:** ₹52,070 (list ₹57,855)
- **When:** 1–31 May
- **Why we're running it:** Keep the April cohort: turn the anniversary buyers who attend into 3-month members.
- **Target:** 7 sales at about ₹52,070 each, ₹3.6L (₹3,64,490) in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** Between half and two-thirds of April 2026 buyers bought again by October. The ones who attend in their first 30 days are the ones worth upgrading, and the freezes answer the summer-travel objection before it is raised.

### Offer 4 of 4: Mother's Day Guilt Trip

- **Type:** Flash sale
- **Who it's for:** Members and anyone buying a 3-Month or bigger
- **Headline:** She carried you for nine months. Carry her to a barre class.
- **What they get:** Buy a 3-Month Unlimited or bigger at the standard price (₹57,855) and get a 4-class pack gift card for your mother (or anyone who raised you), in a card she can actually frame.
- **Price:** ₹57,855
- **When:** 9 May only (24 hours)
- **Why we're running it:** A full-price 3-Month spike plus a new client in every sale.
- **Target:** 1 sales at about ₹57,855 each, ₹57,855 in total
- **Rules:** No stacking with standing perks. Max effective discount 25%.
- **The data behind it:** The gift is a newcomer, not a discount: every card brings a new client into the studio with a buyer who already trains there.
