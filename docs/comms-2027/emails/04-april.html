Guardrails
Ground rules
 

✔︎ No offer above 25% effective discount, bonuses valued at list. Highest: the 9:09 Flash Pack at 24.0%.

✔︎ One offer per purchase. Wheel prizes, flash prices and offers never stack.

✔︎ Average April discount under 15% of gross at every studio (April 2026: KC 30.1%, Bandra 25.3%).

✔︎ Scarcity is real: 9 Club Nine seats per studio, 99 Lease keys per city. Sold out means sold out.

✔︎ No anniversary prices after 30 April, 11:59 pm. The Afterparty rewards attendance, not price.

✔︎ Game prizes not tied to a purchase (Hidden 9s, Plank of Fame, awards) are a marketing cost, budgeted at ₹1.5L.
