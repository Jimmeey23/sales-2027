Strategy
The strategy in five lines
 
1. Sell the habit, not the discount. Every offer sits under a 25% effective-discount cap with bonuses valued at list. We reward commitment (longer memberships, attendance), not price.
2. One hero offer per client type. Each month has one offer for new clients, one for lapsed clients and one for members. Simple for the desk, simple for clients.
3. Peaks with a plan. Four big moments: January intent, the April anniversary (our first ₹1 Cr month), August back-to-routine and the October–November festive and Black Friday window.
4. Usage drives renewal. Clients using 75%+ of their sessions renew at ~80–86%. Challenges, cohorts and the Nine Lives Passport exist to get people into class.
5. Fix the weak months. March, June and November get pre-sales, pause options and sharp short windows so they are not empty.

April
Spotlight: Nine Lives, our 9th anniversary
 

Nine years, nine lives. April becomes a month-long game with a story in three acts: a members-only Pre-Party in late March, a 30-day Party in April where every class can win you something, and an Afterparty in early May that turns April buyers into regulars. Every buyer type gets exactly one hero offer, the biggest money comes from scarce, numbered, near-full-price products, and the fun is free to play, so it fills classes instead of eating margin.

One hero offer per person. The earlier drafts gave the desk 5–9 overlapping April offers. Now: newcomers get Cloud Nine Intro, members renewing get the Lucky Nine Wheel, big spenders get Club Nine or the Nine-Month Lease, lapsed clients get Nine Years, Nine Reasons. Easy to explain in one breath.

Scarcity at full price carries the month. Club Nine (9 numbered Annuals per studio, list price) and the Nine-Month Lease (99 per city) bring ~₹30L of high-ticket revenue at 0–20% effective discount, instead of buying volume at 40%+ like April 2026.

Fun that drives attendance, not discount. The Nine Lives Passport, Hidden 9s, Plank of Fame and the Golden Glute Awards cost little, are free to play and push class visits. Clients using 75%+ of their sessions renew at ~80–86%.

Three acts beat one spike. Pre-Party lifts the weak March, the Party peaks on 9 April (9:09 flash) and 10 April (Birthday Class), and the Afterparty in May protects the repeat rate (target 60–72% vs 41–68% after April 2026).

New revenue lines. Gift Nine gift cards ride into Mother's Day, the Nine Lives merch capsule adds retail, and Squad Goals turns friend groups into 3-Month memberships.

April target ₹1.05 Cr · modelled ₹1.20 Cr. Full plan in the anniversary doc and the April brief.
